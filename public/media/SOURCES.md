# Where these clips come from

Every clip here is trimmed, muted and re-encoded (H.264, no audio) with a
poster frame (`.jpg`) taken from its first frame. Swap any of them for
footage from real Lasan Mart shoots whenever it exists; keep the file names
and nothing else needs to change.
